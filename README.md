Build a beautiful, modern, image-led website for Kongoni Camp Nanyuki, a hospitality destination in Nanyuki, Laikipia, Kenya.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

Kongoni Camp is not exclusively a corporate retreat or conferencing property.

The website should present Kongoni as a versatile destination for:

Corporate retreats and conferences are an important revenue segment, but they should feel like one part of the Kongoni offering, not the identity of the entire property.

The overall positioning should be:

A comfortable base for exploring Nanyuki and Laikipia, meeting, retreating, staying and spending time together.

The website should make sense to someone looking for a room for one night, a family looking for accommodation, a tour operator planning an itinerary, and an organisation looking for a retreat venue.

CONTENT PRINCIPLE

The site must not feel like it is trying to sell one thing to everyone.

Instead, make the property itself the central product.

The visitor should naturally find their path:

I need accommodation → Stay

I'm travelling with a tour operator → Tour Operators

I'm planning a retreat → Meetings & Retreats

I'm travelling with family → Stay / Explore

I'm looking for things to do → Explore Nanyuki

I'm organising a group → Groups

I just want to see the property → Gallery

Every audience should feel welcome without the website becoming cluttered.

MOST IMPORTANT DESIGN IDEA

Build the site around the idea:

Stay. Meet. Explore.

This should connect the three major sides of Kongoni:

STAY

Rooms, suites, rondavels, groups and leisure travellers.

MEET

Conferences, retreats, workshops, training, team-building and events.

EXPLORE

Nanyuki, Mount Kenya, wildlife, conservation, history and Laikipia.

Everything else should support those three pillars.

The result should feel like a real destination website for Kongoni Camp, with enough personality and visual storytelling to make someone want to visit, while still being practical enough for someone who needs to make a booking or organise a programme.
